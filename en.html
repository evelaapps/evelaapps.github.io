Buy, set up, checkmate

Chess where you buy your own pieces.

You get coins and buy your pieces with them: pawn 1, knight and bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9. Then you set them up and play by the normal chess rules until it's mate.

  • Chapters: 11 opponents with quirks, from Intern Ian to Granny Gwen, three stars each.
  • Duel: against the chess computer in 9 levels from 600 to 2650 Elo, with an optional blitz clock.
  • Survival: wave after wave, save coins for the strong opponents.

For iPhone and iPad, in English and German. Free, no sign-up, no ads and no data collection.
