Privacy Policy

for the app Checkmate (German: Schachmatt) and this website

In short: The app does not collect any personal data. There is no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no connection to servers of our own. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Controller

Enes Velagic
Email: evela.apps@gmail.com

Data on your device

Your progress (stars, setups, Elo and record in the duel, best wave and current run in survival, a game in progress) and your settings (sound, board labels, move hints, language, blitz clock) are stored only locally on your device. They are not transmitted. You can delete them in the app under Settings → Progress → Reset, or remove them together with the app.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no notifications, no location, no camera, no microphone, no access to contacts or photos. The chess computer runs on the device itself.

Links from the app

“Help & contact” and “Privacy” in the settings open this website in your browser. The section “This website” applies to that.

Contact by email

If you write to me, I process your email address and your message to reply and to fix reported bugs (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). The mailbox is hosted by Gmail (Google), where the messages are stored. I delete them once they are dealt with and no longer needed.

App Store

The app is offered through Apple's App Store. When you download or update it, Apple processes data under its own responsibility; see Apple's Privacy Policy.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit them, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The pages themselves set no cookies and load no content from other providers.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your data, to data portability and to object. Just send me an email. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
