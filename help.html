How does the game work?

You get coins and buy pieces with them: pawn 1, knight and bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9. You place them on your starting squares and then play by the normal chess rules until a king is checkmated. You can open the guide in the app at any time under Settings → How to play.

What is different from normal chess?

Only the setup: your pieces go on the marked starting squares, and none of them may attack the other king during setup. After that, the normal chess rules apply. Stalemate, threefold repetition and 50 moves without a capture or pawn move are draws.

How do I castle?

Tap your king and then the square two steps to the side (g1 or c1). You can also simply move the king onto the rook or the rook onto the king, and the game does the rest. For that, the rook must already stand on h1 or a1 during setup, neither king nor rook may have moved, the squares between them must be empty, and the king may not be in check or pass over an attacked square.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically and only on your device. A game in progress and your survival run continue after the app restarts. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

How do I delete my progress?

Settings → Progress → Reset, then tap Delete everything once more.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language: Automatic (like your device), Deutsch or English. The game restarts briefly, your progress stays.
